# ADR-019: CI gates on infrastructure, reports owner tests, checks determinism across compilers
- Context: CI must stay green and meaningful while the owner's parts are stubs, and must catch platform-dependent feed generation.
- Decision: one job per preset gates on `ctest -L infra` and writes the owner pass count to the job summary; separate jobs check clang-format, generator output identity across two runs and clang vs GCC, and fuzz the parser for 60 s.
- Alternative rejected: one job running everything with `continue-on-error`.
- Why: per-preset jobs isolate sanitizer failures; a red owner suite would hide infrastructure regressions if it gated.
- Consequence: when the owner suite is complete, flip its step to gating (one line); the smoke benchmark job is added with the benchmark harness.
